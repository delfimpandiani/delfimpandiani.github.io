## Bearing Body Series (2017)

Four-part series that isolates fragments of the bearing (the feminized as potentially pregnant) figure caught mid-gesture, attending to the body as a site of weight, posture, and the quiet labor of holding a/one self together.

- **I: The Bearer (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 100 × 70 cm.
- **II: Ideal Bearing (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 70 × 100 cm.
- **III: Actual Bearing (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 100 × 100 cm.
- **IV: The Beared [From the Belly Button] (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 70 × 70 cm.

## A Flor de Piel Series (2017)


A series of oil canvases attending to skin, surface, and the politics of the body's edges.

- **Pedicure (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 50 × 50 cm.
- **Plena (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 80 × 100 cm.
- **Maciso (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 100 × 100 cm.
- **Y, Bueno (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 80 × 80 cm.
- **Redon's Buddha (2017)** — Acrylic on canvas,  20 x 30 cm.
- **Monochrome (2017)** — Oil on canvas, 50 × 100 cm.
